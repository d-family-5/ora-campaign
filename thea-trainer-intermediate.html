Chapter 1 · Mechanics
Sneak Attack — Getting It Every Single Turn

Intermediate Rogue play isn't complicated — it's disciplined. Your job is to ensure Sneak Attack fires on every turn, no exceptions. Average damage without it is about 7. With it, 10.5. That's 50% more damage, every round, for the whole fight.

The key insight: the ally-adjacent condition is easier to maintain than Advantage, and it works as long as you don't have Disadvantage. This means your positioning choices matter more than any other decision you make.

✓ Reliable SA Sources (maintain these)
Ragnar or Talon adjacent to target · You're within 5 ft (melee) of target plus an ally · Lucian casts Faerie Fire (Advantage) · You're hidden and attacking from stealth
✦ Premium SA Sources (high value)
Hidden attack = Advantage = SA even with no allies near · Faerie Fire = SA + Advantage on attack roll · Prone enemy in melee = Advantage
✗ What Breaks the Ally Condition
You have Disadvantage from any source (poisoned, in darkness, etc.) · No ally within 5 ft of target · You're using a non-finesse melee weapon (not Thea's style but worth knowing)
🗡️ The Sneak Attack Math
Short Bow no SA: avg 7.5 dmg · Short Bow + SA: avg 11 dmg · Rapier + SA: avg 12.5 dmg · Rapier + SA + Crit: avg 19 dmg. Every SA proc is +3.5 free damage.
💡 The Positioning Rule
Before you move, ask: "Where do I stand so that SA fires?" If Ragnar is fighting the Swordsman, you want to be on the opposite side of the Swordsman from Ragnar — he's adjacent, you're adjacent, Sneak Attack fires. This is called flanking and it's Thea's home position every fight.

Chapter 2 · Tactics
Positioning — Where Thea Stands Wins Fights

Thea's 30 ft movement and AC 15 give her flexibility other characters don't have. Where Lucian stays back and casts, Thea dances through the fight — close to trigger SA, then away to avoid retaliation.

⚠️ Thea's Two Dangers
1. Surrounded: Two enemies flanking Thea means two attacks per round at her 9 HP. Always keep one flank free.

2. Opportunity Attacks: Moving OUT of an enemy's melee range triggers a free attack. Solution — after attacking in melee, don't just run. Either Disengage (Action) or don't move away at all.
✓ Best Position
Behind a melee ally, attacking the same target. You get SA (ally adjacent), they absorb hits, you stay mobile. Move in, attack, move back to partial cover.
✦ Hidden Sniper
Use Short Bow from 30-80 ft. Hide behind terrain. Stealth +8 means you're almost invisible. Attack from hiding = Advantage = SA every turn with zero melee risk.
⚡ Hit and Fade
Attack in melee, then Dash away as Bonus Action. Costs your Bonus Action but lets you attack AND reposition without Opportunity Attacks. Works when your turn puts the enemy at 0 HP too.
✗ Don't Do This
Run PAST an enemy to reach another target (Opp. Attack). Charge into a crowd alone (surrounded). Melee attack and then move away without Disengaging (Opp. Attack again).
🧭 Small Size Advantage
Being Small (Halfling) means Thea can move through the space of Medium or larger creatures — Talon and Ragnar are effectively doorways. This is unusually powerful for positioning. Thea can literally run through her allies to reach flanking positions without triggering reactions.

Chapter 3 · Skills
Your Skills Are as Powerful as Your Blades

Thea has the best skill modifiers in the party by a significant margin. Stealth +8 and Acrobatics +8 — with Expertise, that's double proficiency — mean she can attempt things no other character can reliably do. Use this constantly, not just in combat.

👣 Stealth +8 — Expertise
Average roll of 17.5 before the die. Enemies need passive Perception of 18+ to notice. Use before every combat to get Hidden → Advantage on first attack. Also scout ahead, bypass encounters entirely, set up ambushes.
🤸 Acrobatics +8 — Expertise
Escape grapples (Acrobatics vs Grappler's Athletics). Stay standing when pushed. Move through difficult terrain. The DM will use this in creative situations — be ready for "make an Acrobatics check."
🔍 Insight +4, Perception +4
Notice lies (Insight). Spot hidden enemies (Perception, ties to Passive 14). These are the detective skills — before roleplay encounters, roll Insight to read the room. Tell the party what you sense.
🖐 Sleight of Hand +6
Pickpocket, plant evidence, pass items secretly, palming objects. Enormous roleplay value. Your Sailor background means you've used these skills at sea — lean into it narratively.
💡 The Pre-Combat Stealth Setup
When you see combat coming before it starts, ask the DM: "Can Thea attempt to Hide before initiative is rolled?" A Stealth +8 check often succeeds. You start the fight hidden, your first attack has Advantage, and Sneak Attack fires before the enemy even knows where you are. This is the single most powerful thing Thea can do.

Chapter 4 · Judgment
Reading the Fight — When to Stab, When to Vanish

Intermediate Rogue play is about threat assessment. Every turn ask: am I the most dangerous thing in this fight right now, or am I the most at-risk? The answer changes your tactics completely.

🧠 The Three-Question Check
1. Can I get Sneak Attack this turn? If yes — attack. If no — Disengage and reposition, or Hide for next turn's advantage.

2. Will the enemy hit me back? If you're adjacent to two enemies, you'll eat two attacks. Deal damage from the safer target or use Short Bow.

3. Does anyone need saving? You can't heal, but you can redirect attention. Attacking the enemy targeting a downed ally forces it to deal with you.
✓ Attack when...
SA conditions are met · You're attacking from hiding · Target is the biggest threat to your allies · One hit might end the fight (enemy at low HP)
✦ Hide when...
No SA conditions available · You're exposed with low HP · Next round's SA from hiding will deal more than attacking this round without it · You need to reset position
⚡ Disengage when...
You're adjacent to an enemy you don't want to attack · You need to reach a different target · Staying put will get you hit twice next round · An ally needs you to reach them
✗ Avoid...
Attacking without SA when you could set up SA · Running through multiple enemy spaces · Using Short Bow when you're already adjacent (Disadvantage at melee range) · Forgetting Halfling Luck on crucial rolls
🤝 Communicate With Your Party
Tell Talon and Ragnar your plan. "I'm going to flank the archer — can one of you move to the swordsman?" Getting SA requires an ally to be adjacent. Your best allies are the melee fighters. A quick word before you move turns average damage into Sneak Attack every round.

🔑 Key Decisions Recap
Round 1 — Opening: The pre-combat Hide was the highest-value move available. One Action guarantees SA on Turn 2 from safety. Charging in early without SA setup was the common mistake.

Round 2 — Priority target: The Caster is always Priority 1. She deals AoE damage that hits the whole party. One SA hit on a low-CON caster can end her contribution in one turn.

Round 2 — Opportunity Attack trap: Moving past an Enforcer without Disengaging costs you an attack at your 9 HP. The route around the crates is worth the extra movement.

Round 3 — Finishing move: When the Enforcer was at low HP, finishing him with the Rapier freed the whole party from melee pressure. Rogues are excellent finishers on wounded targets.
⚡ Three Habits of Intermediate Rogue Play
1. Pre-combat Hide whenever possible. Stealth +8 almost never fails. It's the best free action in your kit.

2. Know your allies' positions before you move. SA fires when Ragnar or Talon is adjacent. Ask where they're going before committing.

3. Never forget Halfling Luck. On critical saves or attack rolls, that reroll can be the difference between going down and staying up.
