A beginner's guide for Thea Rochiel — Level 1 Rogue
Lesson 1 · Character
Who Is Thea, and How Does She Fight?

Thea is a Halfling Rogue — small, fast, and dangerously precise. She doesn't win fights by being tough. She wins by hitting the right target at the right moment for massive burst damage, then vanishing before anyone can hit back.

Where Lucian uses magic and charm, Thea uses positioning, timing, and opportunism. Her most important ability — Sneak Attack — deals an extra die of damage whenever she has even the slightest edge over an enemy.

❤️ HP: 9 — stay out of danger 🛡 AC: 15 — hard to hit ⚡ Initiative: +4 — usually goes early ✦ Sneak Attack: +1d6 when conditions are met
🗡️ Thea's Strengths
Highest single-hit damage potential in the party · AC 15 (hardest to hit) · Stealth +8 and Acrobatics +8 (best skill character) · DEX +4 means great initiative, attacks, and saves
⚠️ Thea's Vulnerabilities
Only 9 HP — same as the Wizard · No healing, no magic · Can only Sneak Attack ONCE per turn · Does very average damage when Sneak Attack conditions aren't met
🍀 Halfling Luck
If you roll a Natural 1 on an attack, ability check, or saving throw — roll again and use the second roll. The worst possible roll can't stick. This is a passive ability, it just works.
👣 Naturally Stealthy
You can attempt to Hide even when only obscured by a creature one size larger than you. In practice: hide behind your human allies. Halfling-specific and very powerful.
💡 The One Rule to Know
Thea's damage goes from average to exceptional the moment Sneak Attack fires. Your entire combat strategy is about making sure Sneak Attack is available every single turn. Everything else — positioning, weapon choice, movement — exists to serve that goal.

Lesson 2 · Dice
How Rolling Works for Thea

Every attack and skill check uses the same formula: d20 + modifier vs target number. For Thea, that modifier is almost always +4 (DEX) or higher because her whole character is built around Dexterity.

WeaponHit BonusDamageWhen to Use
Rapier+61d8+4Main melee weapon — highest damage die
Short Sword+61d6+4Off-hand or when dual wielding
Dagger+61d4+4Throw at range or off-hand backup
Short Bow+61d6+4Safe ranged option — 80 ft normal range

All four weapons hit with +6 (DEX +4 + Proficiency +2). The choice depends on range and whether Sneak Attack conditions are met.

📖 Example — Attacking the Swordsman
Thea fires her Short Bow at a bandit 30 ft away. She rolls d20 and gets 13, plus her attack bonus of +6 = 19. The bandit's AC is 14. 19 beats 14 — hit! She rolls 1d6+4 = 8 piercing damage. If Sneak Attack fires: add 1d6 = up to 14 damage total.

🍀 Halfling Luck in Action
You roll a Natural 1 on your Stealth check. Normally that's a critical failure. With Halfling Luck it doesn't count — roll again automatically. This applies to attacks too. You literally cannot roll a Natural 1 that sticks.

Lesson 3 · Core Mechanic
Sneak Attack — Thea's Most Important Rule

Sneak Attack adds 1d6 extra damage to one attack per turn. It fires when either of two conditions is met — you don't need both, just one.

✓ Condition 1: Advantage
You roll two d20s and take the higher. Sources: you're hidden, enemy is blinded, Lucian's Faerie Fire, prone enemy in melee, ally uses Help action.
✓ Condition 2: Ally Adjacent
An ally is within 5 ft of the target AND you don't have Disadvantage. The ally doesn't need to do anything — just be standing next to the enemy. This is the easy one.

⚠️ Important Limits
Sneak Attack fires once per turn — not once per attack. If somehow you attack twice, only one attack gets the bonus. Also: it requires a finesse or ranged weapon. A standard longsword doesn't qualify. All of Thea's weapons do.

Lesson 4 · Combat
What You Do Each Turn

Each round you get an Action, a Bonus Action, and 30 ft of Movement. Unlike Lucian who has many options, Thea has a clear priority every turn.

⚔️ Action — Attack
Almost always: attack with your best weapon for the situation. Apply Sneak Attack if conditions are met. This is 90% of your turns.
⚡ Bonus Action — Dash or Off-hand
No Sneak Attack-specific Bonus Action at Level 1. Use it for: Dash (extra 30 ft movement), Help (give ally Advantage), or Off-hand attack with Dagger/Short Sword (no modifier to damage).
🚶 Movement — Positioning
Get into range. After attacking, get OUT of range if possible. Thea doesn't want to eat hits. Use 30 ft intelligently — attack from a flanking position, then step back behind Ragnar.
⚡ Hide (Action)
In the right situation, use your Action to Hide (Stealth +8 — almost guaranteed). Next turn you attack with Advantage = Sneak Attack guaranteed. Great setup turn.
💡 The Simplest Thea Turn
Move to a position adjacent to where Ragnar or Talon is standing next to an enemy.
Action: Attack with Rapier (+6 to hit). Sneak Attack fires automatically because an ally is adjacent.
Bonus Action: Dash away, or hold for emergency off-hand attack.
That's it. Rinse and repeat every round.
🎯 Which Weapon When
Rapier — default melee choice (biggest damage die, 1d8)
Short Bow — enemy too far away, or you need to stay safe at range
Dagger — thrown at 20/60 ft, or off-hand second attack
Short Sword — paired with Dagger for dual wield option

📋 Thea's Turn Checklist
1. Can I Sneak Attack? (ally adjacent OR I have Advantage)
2. Am I in a safe position? (not surrounded)
3. Attack with the right weapon for the range

⬆ Going First is a Big Deal for Thea
With Initiative +4, Thea often acts before enemies. On Round 1, before any ally is adjacent, you may not have easy Sneak Attack conditions. Solution: use your first turn to Hide (Action, Stealth +8) — then Round 2 you attack with Advantage = guaranteed Sneak Attack.
